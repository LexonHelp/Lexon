Lexon Privacy Policy

Effective date: 20 October 2026
Publisher and responsible party: WGR, South Africa
Contact: Lexon.Help@gmail.com

This Privacy Policy explains how Lexon (the "App"), a Windows desktop application for text expansion and AI-powered writing assistance, handles your information. It applies to the App as distributed through the Microsoft Store.

1. The short version

  • Lexon runs on your device. Your settings, snippets, and learned vocabulary are stored on your device.
  • We (WGR) do not operate servers that receive your typing, and we do not collect your text.
  • Text leaves your device only when you use a cloud AI feature, and then it goes directly to the AI provider you chose, using your own account or API key.
  • You can use Lexon with a local model, or turn cloud features off entirely with Local-only mode.
  • Lexon does not show advertising and does not sell your information.

2. What Lexon accesses on your device

To offer suggestions, expansions, grammar checks, and rewrites, Lexon needs access to:

  • Your keystrokes while the App is running, through a global keyboard hook, so it can recognise what you are typing and trigger expansions;
  • The text of the field you are typing in, so it can work out the current word and offer a suggestion in context;
  • Text you select, when you ask it to rewrite or check that text;
  • The name of the active application, so it can apply per-app settings and respect your blocklist;
  • The clipboard, only when you use the setup step that detects an API key you have just copied.

Lexon does not intentionally operate in password or other secure fields, or in applications you have blocked. These safeguards are intended to reduce risk but may not catch every case, so please block any application where you do not want Lexon active.

3. What is stored on your device

The following is stored locally and is not sent to us:

  • your settings and preferences;
  • snippets and expansions you create;
  • learned vocabulary, writing-style information, and statistics that help Lexon personalise its suggestions;
  • your list of blocked applications;
  • an activity log that records which cloud AI provider was used and when, without storing the text that was sent;
  • your AI provider choice and API key, if you enter one.

You can export and import your personalization data, and you can reset or delete it from within the App.

4. When information leaves your device

Cloud AI features

If you choose a cloud AI provider and use a feature that needs it (for example, rewriting selected text), Lexon sends the relevant text and instructions to that provider. Depending on the feature this can include the text you selected, the text around your cursor, and your chosen tone or style settings. The App can ask you before sending text to a cloud provider, and you can control this in Settings.

This happens directly between your device and the provider, using your own account or API key. We do not receive a copy and we do not see the text.

Local models

If you use a locally run model, your text is processed on your device or on a network location you configure. It does not go to a cloud provider.

Local-only mode

When Local-only mode is on, Lexon does not send your text to any cloud AI provider.

Microsoft

The Microsoft Store handles your download, any payment, and its own diagnostic and usage data under Microsoft's privacy statement. We do not control that processing.

5. Third-party AI providers

When you use a cloud provider, the provider becomes responsible for the text it receives, under its own terms and privacy policy. Providers may log, retain, or use submitted data according to their own policies and your account settings. Please read the policy of any provider you connect, and choose Local-only mode or a local model if you do not want your text to leave your device.

6. Information you send to us

We collect only what you choose to send us, for example your email address and message when you contact Lexon.Help@gmail.com. We use it to reply and to improve the App, and we do not share it except where the law requires.

7. How we use information

The App uses the information in Section 2 and Section 3 on your device to provide its features, to personalise suggestions to you, and to apply your privacy settings. We do not use your text for advertising, we do not build profiles of you on our servers, and we do not sell or rent your information.

8. Security

Your data is stored on your device, so its security also depends on your device and Windows account. Please keep your device, your account, and your API keys secure. No software can be guaranteed completely secure, and we cannot guarantee the security of data after it reaches a third-party provider.

9. Retention and deletion

Local data stays on your device until you delete it. You can reset or delete learned data from the App's settings, and uninstalling the App removes the App itself. If any data remains in the App's data folder after uninstalling, you can delete that folder manually. For anything held by a third-party provider, contact that provider. For emails you have sent us, you can ask us to delete them using the contact address below.

10. International transfers

Cloud AI providers may process your text in countries other than South Africa, including countries with different data protection laws. By choosing to use a cloud provider, you direct that transfer. Using Local-only mode or a local model avoids it.

11. Children

Lexon is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

12. Your rights

We are the responsible party for any personal information you send us directly, under South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA). You may ask us to confirm what personal information we hold about you, to correct or delete it, or to stop using it, by emailing Lexon.Help@gmail.com. Depending on where you live, you may have similar rights under other data protection laws.

If you believe we have handled your personal information unlawfully, you may complain to the Information Regulator of South Africa (inforegulator.org.za). We would appreciate the chance to resolve your concern first.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date at the top shows when it was last changed. We will publish updates at the same public location and, for material changes, tell you in the App or the Microsoft Store listing.

14. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact Lexon.Help@gmail.com.
