Lexon Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

Lexon is a Windows typing assistant published by WGR ("we", "us"). You can reach us at Lexon.Help@gmail.com.

The short version

  • Lexon works on your PC. We do not run servers that receive your typing, your settings, or any information about you.
  • Lexon has no accounts, no analytics, no telemetry, and does not send crash reports.
  • Text only leaves your PC if you connect a cloud AI provider (OpenAI, Gemini, or DeepSeek) or a non-local Ollama server, and then use a feature that sends text to it. Lexon asks before each kind of send by default.
  • Lexon skips password fields, fields it cannot confirm are safe, and apps you block.

What Lexon reads while you use it

Lexon has to read some text to help you. It does this on your PC:

  • Text you type in other apps. The current word and the words around it are held in memory to offer suggestions, spelling fixes, and grammar fixes. Lexon does not read text from password fields or from apps on your blocked list.
  • Text you select, only when you ask for a rewrite (or turn on preparing a rewrite as soon as you select text).
  • The name of the app you are typing in, so your blocked-app list and per-app writing tone can apply.
  • Your clipboard, briefly and only on request. If you choose Get your API key, Lexon watches the clipboard for a few minutes for a key that matches that provider. It ignores anything else, and nothing from the clipboard is logged or sent.

What Lexon stores on your PC

Everything below stays in %LocalAppData%\Lexon on your computer:

  • Your settings, blocked apps, text expansions, and custom terminology.
  • Words Lexon has learned from your typing and your writing-style profile. You can turn learning off, never learn a word, delete learned words, and export or import this data.
  • Recent edits (optional): the text Lexon replaced and the text it put in its place, up to 200 characters each, for the last 20 edits, so you can undo. Turn this off or clear it in Settings → Check.
  • A cloud activity log that records which provider, which app, and what kind of action, but never the text itself.
  • Diagnostic files such as placement.log and crash.log, written on your PC for troubleshooting. They are never uploaded.

Lexon protects its stored profile data with AES-GCM encryption, with the key protected by Windows DPAPI. Your API key is stored locally and protected with DPAPI. We never receive it.

Cloud AI (optional and off until you set it up)

Cloud AI only works if you add your own provider key (or choose a non-local Ollama server). When a provider is connected, Lexon may send:

  • The words around your cursor, only if Send words around the cursor to cloud AI while I type is on. It is off by default.
  • The text you selected for a rewrite, plus your rewrite instruction and any tone or writing-style hint, only when you ask for a rewrite and Allow AI rewrites of selected text is on. It does not send the rest of the document.
  • Selected text before you choose a rewrite, only if Prepare a rewrite as soon as I select text is on. It is off by default.

By default, Ask before sending text to AI is on. Lexon names the provider and what would be sent, and lets you send once, always allow that kind of send, or not send.

Requests go straight from your PC to the provider you chose. They do not pass through any server of ours. Local-only mode stops all AI, including Ollama on your PC, immediately and keeps your saved key. Ollama on your own PC counts as local. An Ollama server on another machine is treated like a cloud send.

Once text reaches a provider, that provider's own privacy policy and retention rules apply, not ours. You may also be charged by the provider.

Updates

If you installed Lexon from the Microsoft Store, updates come through the Store. If you installed a direct download, Lexon may ask GitHub which version is newest when it starts. That request contains no typing data. You can turn the startup check off in Settings → General. Local-only mode does not turn it off.

Sharing

We do not sell or share your information, because we do not have it. Text only goes to a cloud AI provider if you set one up, as described above. We may be required to respond to a lawful request, but we hold no data about you to hand over.

Your choices and your data

Your data lives on your PC, so you control it:

  • Change or turn off learning, recent edits, and each AI option in Settings.
  • Block any app from Lexon in Settings → Privacy or Apps.
  • Export or import your settings and learned data, or clear them.
  • To remove everything, uninstall Lexon and then delete the folder %LocalAppData%\Lexon. Uninstalling alone leaves that folder behind.

Because we do not collect or hold personal information about you, there is nothing on our side to access, correct, or delete. If you believe the law gives you further rights, contact us at the address above.

Children

Lexon is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top and describe material changes in the app's release notes.
